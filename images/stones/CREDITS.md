that cannot say where its product photography came from has a problem no
licence fixes.

## Still not photographed

`princess`, `pear`, `marquise` and `radiant` have no file here and fall back to
the drawn plates. The free libraries have almost no loose stones in those cuts
— nearly every result is a ring, a pendant or a hand — and a ring photo on a
loose-stone catalogue is worse than a drawing.

## These are stand-ins

Not one of these photographs is of a Triple 7 stone. They show the cut, and the
product page says so. Before anything is genuinely sold, each stone needs its
own photograph with `products.image_url` set. Naming and shooting notes are in
`COMMERCE.md`, under Photography.
