The Site

Land At Sepelong Game Farm

Sepelong is a land asset held by the group and run as a game farm. It sits outside the production chain, but inside the same commitment: land in our care should be left in better condition than we found it.

The farm supports the environmental side of our work — lowering the footprint of the wider operation, and giving small business owners in the area a way to trade with us that is not tied to a pit.
